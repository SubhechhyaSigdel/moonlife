Meet the Founder

The vision behind Moonlife

Jeevan Basyal

Founder & Owner

Jeevan Basyal is a visionary youth entrepreneur dedicated to making a positive impact in his community. With a passion for quality products and sustainable business practices, he founded Moonlife to serve others while contributing to the local economy.

His mission is to provide pure, quality kirana items and mustard oil that bring value to every household and wholesale shop. Jeevan believes in doing something meaningful in his own land and serving others with integrity and excellence.
